                                                                Technical Specification

 Equipment :Multifunctional Labour Cot Type I


Multifunctional Labor Cot  Type I

1. Should have a table top with all edges smoothly finished with a foldable protective side rails. The cot shall be completely made up of
SS 304.

2. Should have a dimension of L 190 X W 90 X H 80 cm

3. Should be able to hold minimum of 200kg.

4. Should have three sections top. Each section should have separate removable moulded PU form mattress (Velcro should not be used
for fixing with table top)

5. Back rest should be adjustable on ratchet.

6. Should have a removable SS collection tray at a distance not exceeding 25 cm from the U-Cut (Table top). The table top section
should have slope all around the U-Cut for easy drainage of fluids into the collection tray. The diameter of the collection tray shall be at
least 40 cm and depth at least 15 - 20 cm.

7. The U cut should have a diameter of 35 cm and width of this section is 50 cm.

8. Leg section should be foldable or telescopic under the mainframe completely for lithotomy positions. To and fro motion of the leg
section should be very smooth

9. Should have side rails for fixing accessories across the length of the table top on both sides. Should have S.S. head rest with moulded
mattresses, firm removable side railing steel hand grips, a pair of PU moulded SS adjustable Lithotomy rod with leg crutches and
support for foot.

10. Should provide with a detachable saline stand having a provision for fixing four positions (Head end and middle position) on the
cot. 2 Nos of IV stand should be provided.

11. Base should be mounted on four heavy duty rubber shoes. At least 4 inches length
